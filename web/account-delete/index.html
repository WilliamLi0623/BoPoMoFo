Delete your Bopomofo account

You can delete a signed-in account in the app under Settings → Account → Delete account.

Deletion removes your cloud profile, synced learning events, device records, review snapshots, confusion data, settings, sync state, and authentication identity. It cannot be undone. Learning data stored on a device is separate and is removed only when you select the local-data deletion option in the app.
