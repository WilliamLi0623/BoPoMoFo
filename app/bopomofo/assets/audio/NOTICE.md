# Offline pronunciation audio

The packaged pronunciation clips `F_ba.mp3` and `F_ma.mp3` come from the
Ministry of Digital Affairs' CNS11643 Full Character Library.

- Official archive: https://www.cns11643.gov.tw/opendata/Voice.zip
- Official release index: https://www.cns11643.gov.tw/opendata/release.txt
- `Voice.zip` version listed in the release index: `20250113`
- Archive entries: `female.zip/F_ba.mp3`, `female.zip/F_ma.mp3`
- License: Government Open Data License, Version 1.0
- License text: https://data.gov.tw/license

The official archive index maps `ba` to `ㄅㄚ` and `ma` to `ㄇㄚ`. These two
MP3 files are bundled unchanged for offline playback.
